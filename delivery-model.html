Scoping

One page. Fixed price.

The scoping call is free and takes 30 minutes.

If the work is ready to build, you get a one-page statement of work. If it isn't — because the data isn't there, or nobody can say what “done” means — we'll say so and suggest what would make it ready.

Gates

Five gates. Each one is a decision.

  1. 01

    Scope

    The job, the users, the systems, the risks, the smallest useful outcome.

  2. 02

    Design

    Data flow, model and retrieval choices, integrations, controls and acceptance tests.

  3. 03

    Build

    Working demos every one to two weeks. For software, the agentic gates apply to every change.

  4. 04

    Accept

    Tested against the written criteria, real examples and edge cases. You sign off.

  5. 05

    Run

    Monitored, reviewed and improved — by us or by your team.

Standards

Documented handover

Workflow map, data notes, runbook, known limitations.

Run

Systems change after launch. We keep them working.

Models are updated, documents go stale, usage grows and costs drift. Run is the follow-on to any build: we watch it, test it, and make the small changes that keep it useful. Larger changes are scoped separately.

Compliance

Australian obligations, designed in.

The obligations that apply in Australia, and the records our builds keep for each.

Privacy Act APP 1.7–1.9 — from 10 Dec 2026 Federal adoption guidance (Oct 2025) ISO/IEC 42001 alignment
01

Privacy Act — automated decisions (from 10 December 2026)

APPs 1.7–1.9 require your privacy policy to describe the kinds of personal information used, and the kinds of decisions made, when a computer program makes or substantially assists a decision that could significantly affect someone's rights or interests. Our builds document exactly that: what the system decides, what personal information it uses, and where a person reviews it.

02

Federal adoption guidance (October 2025)

We design to the Australian Government's Guidance for AI Adoption (October 2025). Our gates and Run reviews map to it.

03

ISO/IEC 42001

If you are working toward certification against the management system standard, we produce the records it expects: system descriptions, risk notes, test evidence and change logs. We don't certify; we make certification evidence easier to gather.

04

Data residency named up front

Before we build, we tell you where each service stores and processes your data, and we use Australian regions where the service offers them.

05

Audit trail

Inputs, sources, prompt and model versions, approvals and outputs are logged for every run, retained to your policy.
