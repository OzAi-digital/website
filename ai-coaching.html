The 90-day rollout

Five stages, from baseline to habit.

Each stage leaves something in place, so the change holds after the sessions end.

  1. 01

    Baseline

    Current tools, confidence, data risks and priority work.

  2. 02

    Set up

    Approved tools, usage rules, starter prompts, manager expectations.

  3. 03

    Train

    Role-based sessions for executives, managers, staff and champions.

  4. 04

    Practise

    Coaching inside real work until it's repeatable and reviewable.

  5. 05

    Embed

    Playbooks, office hours, champion routines, adoption measures.
