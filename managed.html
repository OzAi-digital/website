Why Managed AI?

AI isn't set-and-forget. Models drift, costs creep, new capabilities emerge weekly. You need a partner who monitors, maintains, and continuously improves your AI systems. We're that partner.

Operating Rhythm

AI systems need regular attention because source data, model behaviour, usage patterns, and business rules change.

Weekly Checks

For active workflows: error patterns, failed runs, queue build-up, provider outages, access issues, and unusual cost spikes.

Monthly Report

Plain-English summary of usage, costs, notable failures, prompt changes, retrieval issues, user feedback, and recommended fixes.

Quarterly Review

Review whether the workflow still matches the business process, whether the stack is still fit for purpose, and what should be improved next.

Change Control

Prompts, models, retrieval settings, connected tools, and security settings are changed with notes and rollback awareness.

Source Freshness

Knowledge assistants and RAG workflows are checked for stale documents, broken ingestion, missing metadata, and outdated source sets.

Adoption Feedback

User issues, confusing outputs, repeated edits, and new workflow ideas are collected so support improves actual use, not just infrastructure.

What Is Included

Managed AI support is practical maintenance and improvement, with larger changes scoped separately.

Included

Monitoring reviews, small prompt edits, broken integration checks, model routing adjustments, documentation updates, and support for agreed workflows.

Scoped Separately

New workflows, major integrations, new user portals, large data migrations, governance redesign, or significant rebuilds.

Client-Owned

Cloud accounts, source data, user approvals, business decisions, and final sign-off on any AI-assisted output.
