OzAI company enablement

AI training should match the maturity of the business.

We help companies move from scattered experimentation to safe, measurable, workflow-level AI adoption.

The adoption gap

Most teams have AI access before they have AI capability.

People try tools individually, but the organisation has not agreed what good use looks like.

Leaders want productivity, while risk teams worry about data, accuracy, and accountability.

Generic prompt training creates interest, but rarely changes how work is done.

AI maturity levels

Each stage needs a different teaching strategy.

0

Blocked

Teach executive context, safe starter uses, and risk literacy.

1

Experimenting

Teach prompting, data handling, tool choice, and output review.

2

Standardising

Teach team playbooks, champion routines, and adoption metrics.

3

Operationalising

Teach workflow redesign, RAG, integrations, and evaluation.

4

Scaling

Teach AI portfolio governance, agent supervision, and internal academies.

What we can teach

Five learning tracks cover the roles that make adoption work.

Executives

AI reality check, investment choices, governance posture, and priority domains.

Managers

Use-case spotting, workflow selection, team routines, and adoption measurement.

Staff

Safe prompting, drafting, summarising, research, analysis, and review habits.

Champions

Peer coaching, playbook ownership, clinic facilitation, and pattern capture.

Technical teams

RAG, APIs, secure integrations, evaluations, workflow agents, and AI-assisted delivery.

Appropriate by maturity

Do not teach advanced automation before the basics are safe.

Blocked Leadership briefing, AI myths, risk framing, approved starter uses
Experimenting Prompting foundations, data rules, review checklist, tool comparison
Standardising Team playbooks, workflow labs, champion network, metrics
Operationalising RAG, integrations, automation design, evaluation, human-in-the-loop controls
Scaling Portfolio governance, agent supervision, train-the-trainer, operating model redesign
Rollout model

A coaching sprint turns training into changed work.

  1. AssessMaturity, tools, data, workflows, and sponsor goals.
  2. AlignExecutives and managers agree guardrails and pilot domains.
  3. TrainRole-based workshops build safe, practical AI capability.
  4. CoachTeams redesign real workflows and test them in the field.
  5. TransferChampions inherit playbooks, clinics, and adoption metrics.

Recommended first conversation

Start with maturity, not tools.

A 60-minute discovery session should identify the audience, current maturity, approved tools, sensitive data boundaries, and two candidate workflows for a coaching sprint.
