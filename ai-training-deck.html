OzAI company AI training

From zero AI tooling to confident, safe AI users.

A practical training journey for companies that want staff using AI well: clear tools, safe habits, role-based practice, and workflow-level adoption.

Open the KB
The starting point

A company starting from zero needs more than tool access.

Staff may not know which tools are approved, what data is safe, or when AI output needs human review.

Leaders want productivity, but need practical rules before AI becomes part of normal work.

One-off prompt training creates interest, but does not create confident users or consistent practices.

AI journey

The path is from no tooling to confident, governed use.

0

No tooling

Agree business goals, approved tools, data boundaries, and first safe use cases.

1

First access

Teach AI basics, prompting foundations, data handling, and review habits.

2

Safe use

Build staff confidence with approved tasks, examples, and team playbooks.

3

Team practice

Coach teams through real workflows, quality checks, and repeatable routines.

4

Confident users

Champions, managers, and staff know when to use AI, how to review it, and when to escalate.

What we teach

Training is role-based because adoption is a company habit.

Executives

AI reality check, business priorities, governance posture, and investment choices.

Managers

Use-case spotting, workflow selection, review routines, and adoption measurement.

Staff

Safe prompting, drafting, summarising, research, analysis, and output checking.

Champions

Peer coaching, playbook ownership, office hours, and reusable pattern capture.

Appropriate by stage

Do not teach advanced automation before everyday use is safe.

No tooling Leadership briefing, approved tools, data rules, starter use cases, rollout plan
First access AI basics, prompting foundations, privacy habits, output review checklist
Safe use Role-based examples, team playbooks, manager guidance, practice labs
Team practice Workflow coaching, shared templates, quality checks, champion office hours
Confident users Adoption rhythm, internal champions, advanced workflows, governance review
Rollout model

A 90-day journey turns training into working practice.

  1. BaselineUnderstand current tools, confidence, data risks, and priority workflows.
  2. Set upAgree approved tools, usage rules, starter prompts, and manager expectations.
  3. TrainRun role-based workshops for executives, managers, staff, and champions.
  4. PracticeCoach teams inside real workflows until AI use becomes repeatable and reviewable.
  5. EmbedTransfer playbooks, office hours, champion routines, and adoption reporting.

Recommended first conversation
