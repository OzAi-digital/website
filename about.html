Principles

What we hold ourselves to.

  1. 01

    People stay accountable.

    Software and workflows make people faster; they don't take decisions away from the people responsible for them.

  2. 02

    Test it or don't build it.

    If we can't write the acceptance test, the work isn't ready.

  3. 03

    Plain over clever.

    Plain code where rules will do; models where language or judgement is genuinely needed.

  4. 04

    Leave you capable.

    Source, documentation and runbooks are yours. We'd rather be rehired than relied on.

Boundaries

What we don't do.

  • Open-ended strategy roadmaps without a build attached
  • Results promises we can't test
  • Merges without human approval
