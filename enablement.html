Practical Capability Gains

Training is measured by whether staff can use AI safely on real work, not by generic productivity claims.

Draft Staff can draft, critique, and refine emails, reports, summaries, and briefs with clear review habits
Verify Teams know how to check sources, identify hallucinations, and avoid using AI for unsupported claims
Protect Sensitive data, client information, and confidential material are handled under approved rules
Reuse Reusable prompt patterns and workflow templates are captured for common team tasks
Adopt Managers get guidance for embedding AI into operating rhythms, reviews, and team norms
Escalate Teams learn when to stop, seek review, or move a use case into a governed workflow build
