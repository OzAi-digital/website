Use case deck

AI catalogue chat for fridge specifications.

A fridge company needed fast, accurate lookup across changing product data. We indexed the catalogue and built a chatbot that answers from current approved specifications.

View AI Workflow Builds
The operational problem

Product knowledge was scattered, slow to search, and easy to age.

Sales and support teams had to search through PDFs, spreadsheets, web pages, and supplier files to answer simple specification questions.

Old catalogue copies created risk: the model number could be right, but the dimensions, energy rating, or available finish could be wrong.

Customers wanted quick comparisons and compatibility checks, not a link to another document.

The build

Vector indexing plus a chatbot grounded in the live catalogue.

Catalogue ingestionProduct PDFs, spreadsheets, and structured feeds are parsed into searchable records.
Vector storageSpecifications, model names, aliases, and feature text are embedded with product metadata.
Grounded chatThe assistant retrieves current records first, then answers with the relevant product context.
Source awarenessAnswers can include model references, source files, catalogue version, and update date.
Admin updatesNew catalogue data can be re-indexed without rebuilding the customer experience.
Fallback behaviorWhen current data is missing, the chatbot says so instead of guessing from old knowledge.
How it works

The answer path is designed around current product data.

  1. Update catalogueUpload or sync approved product files and structured data.
  2. Extract specificationsParse dimensions, capacity, energy rating, finish, warranty, and install notes.
  3. Index with metadataEmbed product content with model, category, source, version, and active status.
  4. Retrieve current matchesChat questions search the index and filter out retired or superseded records.
  5. Answer with controlsThe model responds only when the retrieved catalogue context supports the answer.
What users can ask

The useful questions are concrete and product-specific.

Find a model

"Which integrated fridge fits a 900 mm cavity with left hinge?"

Compare products

"Compare these three models by capacity, energy rating, and finish."

Check install details

"What clearance is needed behind this model?"

Find replacement

"What is the closest current replacement for this discontinued fridge?"

Confirm accessories

"Which trim kit or warranty option applies to this product?"

Freshness and accuracy

The control is simple: no current source, no confident answer.

Versioned catalogue Each record knows which source file, feed, and publication date it came from.
Active product filter Retired and superseded products are excluded from normal answers unless specifically requested.
Scheduled re-indexing Updates refresh the searchable catalogue so answers track approved product data.
Answer constraints The chatbot refuses or asks for clarification when retrieved context is insufficient.
Review loop Unanswered or low-confidence questions become a queue for catalogue cleanup.
Lean delivery stack

Cloudflare and Google Cloud first, with vendor choice by workload.

FrontendCloudflare Pages for the catalogue chat experience.
API runtimeCloudflare Workers for retrieval, auth checks, and model orchestration.
StorageR2 or Google Cloud Storage for approved source files and parsed artefacts.
IndexCloudflare Vectorize, Vertex AI Vector Search, or pgvector depending on scale.
ModelsGemini, OpenAI, or Claude selected for cost, latency, and answer quality.
IntegrationsOptional ecommerce, CRM, PIM, support desk, or website handoff.
Business outcome

A product assistant that keeps up with the catalogue.

The result is faster lookup for staff, fewer stale answers, cleaner catalogue operations, and a path to customer-facing product chat once the internal workflow is trusted.
