Reference design

AI voice receptionist for a local surgery.

Patients can start on the web, continue by phone or email, verify by SMS, book appointments, and get help in English or Chinese.

View AI Workflow Builds
The front-desk pressure

Reception teams lose time to repeat calls, missed calls, and channel switching.

Patients call for opening hours, fees, appointment availability, preparation instructions, and follow-up questions.

Context is often lost when a patient starts in web chat, later calls the surgery, then asks for confirmation by email.

Language barriers make simple admin work harder for both patients and reception staff.

The build

One receptionist agent across web, phone, SMS, and email.

Voice and textLatest voice and text models handle spoken and written patient conversations.
Appointment bookingThe agent checks availability, collects required details, and books or changes appointments.
Bilingual supportPatients can speak or type naturally in English or Chinese.
Twilio verificationSMS verification confirms the patient before sensitive admin actions.
Conversation continuityWeb, phone, and email share the same case context where consent and identity checks allow.
Human handoffThe workflow routes urgent, clinical, or uncertain cases to staff.
Patient journey

The patient should not have to restart the conversation.

  1. Starts on webThe patient asks about booking, fees, or preparation instructions.
  2. Verifies by SMSTwilio sends a code before appointment changes or account-specific details.
  3. Books a timeThe agent checks available slots and confirms the booking rules.
  4. Continues by phoneIf the patient calls later, the agent can recover the recent context.
  5. Confirms by emailThe final summary, instructions, or booking confirmation is sent through the right channel.
Reception capabilities

Start with high-volume admin work, not clinical judgement.

Book

Find suitable appointment times and collect required booking information.

Change

Reschedule or cancel appointments after verification and policy checks.

Explain

Opening hours, fees, location, parking, forms, and preparation instructions.

Collect

Reason for visit, preferred practitioner, language needs, and contact details.

Escalate

Urgent symptoms, clinical questions, complaints, and low-confidence answers go to humans.

Safety and privacy

The agent is a receptionist, not a doctor.

No diagnosis The agent does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.
Urgent escalation Emergency language and risky symptoms trigger clear escalation to emergency care or staff.
Identity checks SMS verification and matching rules are required before account-specific actions.
Minimum data The workflow collects only what is needed for the admin task and records the handoff trail.
Human control Staff can review conversations, tune approved answers, and override automation rules.
Lean delivery stack

Cloudflare and GCP orchestration with Twilio at the channel layer.

Voice and SMSTwilio Voice and SMS for calls, verification, and phone routing.
Web chatCloudflare Pages or existing website embed for the start of the conversation.
OrchestrationCloudflare Workers coordinate identity, state, tools, and model calls.
ModelsLatest voice and text models selected for latency, quality, and cost.
SchedulingIntegration with the surgery appointment system or a controlled booking queue.
EmailConfirmation, summaries, and staff handoff through the practice's email workflow.
Business outcome

Better access for patients and less repeat admin for staff.

The first release should reduce missed calls, cover after-hours admin questions, support Chinese-speaking patients, and keep reception focused on in-clinic work and sensitive cases.
